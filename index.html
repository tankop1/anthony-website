About

“Enjoy the best things in your life ’cause you ain’t gonna get to live it twice.” Mac Miller

I really resonate with this quote as it made me realize that if I am going to live one life, I might as well live it trying to conquer my dreams. My name is Anthony Luparello, and I am a senior film student currently enrolled at the University of Texas at Austin; it goes without saying that I absolutely love the art of video editing.

Whether it be films, commercials, or all of the above, I love flaunting my creativity through visual means in order to help inspire and create emotions in the people watching. Beyond editing, I am also really into videography, assistant directing, script supervision, and social media management.

If you’d like to collaborate, get in touch below or email me at antluparello@gmail.com.
